# UI Patterns and Reusable Widget Library

This is the authoritative reusable widget library for Idle Wizard. Before adding UI, map every visible or interactive building block to an entry here and reuse its contract.

## Reuse Rule

- Classify each building block as `reuse`, `extension`, or `new widget` before editing product UI code.
- `reuse` uses a library entry unchanged. `extension` supplies feature data, copy, or composition without changing the entry's visual, interaction, state, or accessibility contract.
- `new widget` includes a new primitive, compound component, scroll behavior, box/dialog type, control pattern, or a meaningfully different variant of an existing entry.
- Before adding a new widget, identify its exact name, purpose, closest existing entry and why it is insufficient, supported states, and intended consumers.
- Implement new widgets as reusable project components and add them to this library with their stable source, contract, and real-app preview evidence.
- Every completed UI task reports `New widgets: none` or lists the widgets introduced.

### Automated Garden Plot

`GardenPlotWidget` automated variant in
`src/rendering/pixi/pages/garden/GardenPixiPage.js`; standalone
`compound.automated-garden-plot` integration in
`src/rendering/pixi/pages/garden/GardenWidgets.ui-editor.js`.

This wide Garden production bed appears after that plot's combined automation
research. It occupies one complete grid row. The long soil's left edge aligns
with the first normal plot's visible soil edge and covers roughly two and a
half normal plots. A right-side `70x78px` control block aligns its outer edge
with the third normal plot: the seed selector spans the top, while `Auto` and
`xN` share the lower row. `Auto` and `xN` reuse Brewing's `32x36px` visual
geometry and `44px` invisible tap targets; the Auto gear is `27px` high in
both rooms. All gaps are `6px`. The selector
raises the seed pack above the selected seed name, and `xN` centers against the
visible button face rather than the image bounds. The progress
rail remains directly beneath the soil instead of extending under the controls.
Seed choice is per plot and may change the next automated crop while another
crop is active. Auto Off uses yellow and Auto On uses green, matching Brewing.
`xN` cycles from 1 through the researched planting multiplier for the next
cycle; an active crop preserves its committed harvest. Render one
plant per committed unit, capped at five. Each visible one-to-five-plant group
is evenly spaced and centered on the long soil. Automated plants sit `2px`
above the manual baseline with deterministic per-plot/per-slot vertical
variance capped at `1px`; placement remains stable across ticks and rebinds.
The soil bed retains the normal plot
timer, progress, notification, and reduced-motion contracts. An accepted
timer-reduction tap resolves the nearest visible herb from the release point
and applies the snap, spark burst, and removed-time label to that herb only;
the soil and sibling herbs remain still while the cooldown stays plot-wide.
Nested controls own their own release targets. States: Auto on/off, empty, growing
x1-x5, ready, harvesting, future seed selected, active quantity committed, reduced
motion, pooled reset, and destroyed. Scope: researched Garden plot automation.
Harvest completion renders one herb drop per authoritative harvested unit with
a five-drop visual cap; manual plots keep their single visible plant while
automated beds distribute those drops across their visible plant slots.
Reproducible preview: `src/dev/uiRecipes/garden-plots.html?automated=1`.
Evidence: `screenshots/garden-automated-plot-390x844.jpg`,
`screenshots/garden-automated-plot-native-crop.jpg`, and
`screenshots/garden-automated-plot-desktop-fitted.jpg`. Single-tile proportion
and color parity: `screenshots/garden-automated-plot-reference-review.html`.
