# Workshop Page

The Workshop is the first room view.

It sits between Garden and Research. It shows one `Elara's Request` at a time, plus the summon action, bag popup, transient reward flyouts, discoveries, leaderboard, alliance controls, and page name. Completing the final request advances the player level automatically; the progression slot never becomes a separate level-up action. Segmented request progress and completion flights live in the shared top panel alongside mana, coin, crystal, and level. The first-run reveal gate keeps that progress row hidden until Elara introduces requests. Prestige is its own gated page after level 7. Workshop secondary actions unlock by player level; keep those gates in page managers and keep gameplay effects behind gameplay/backend facades.

Follow `docs/style.md`: high-contrast text, quiet surfaces, readable Lilita One text, simple non-dialog boxes, and no decorative visuals. Reuse shared row, popup, border-label, and expandable-box patterns from `docs/ui-patterns.md`.

The Workshop side controls form two fixed top-aligned stacks directly below `Elara's Request`. Every control declares only a `left`/`right` side and numeric weight; the retained page sorts visible controls by weight and packs them into fixed source-space slots without leaving gaps for hidden controls. Visibility changes fade, slide from the owned edge, and settle at the fixed slot in at most `200ms`; reduced motion snaps directly to the final layout. `Stats` is the first right-side icon action, while `Bag` uses the dedicated backpack art in the left-side stack. Both reuse the `45.5x80.25px` icon-over-label action geometry and compact press feedback. The Stats report uses one dominant pie chart and two data rows so it cannot collapse into a wireless signal glyph at HUD scale; its warm parchment face, single muted-teal data accent, compact lower-right shadow, and thick black outline keep the tiny icon clean without repeating the Bag's orange leather fill. `Tasks` uses a compact cream-and-tan quest scroll with one muted-red check seal, the same upper-left light and lower-right shadow, and the same thick black contour at the shared icon size. Workshop side-control labels use Title Case with white fill and the shared black text stroke. `Inbox` uses a matching warm-brown courier owl holding a rolled message, with smooth fills, compact shadows, and heavy black strokes. `Leaderboard` uses the matching restrained golden, black-outlined trophy art. `Discoveries` uses the matching leather journal, magnifying glass, and gold-corner art. `Summon Seed` uses an `11px` action label on the shorter cost-button background configuration recolored to the shared info icon's blue/cyan palette, with black-stroked action and cost text plus the shared mana-drop icon and numeric cost on its second row. Its `92x52px` cost button sits `26px` lower than the fixed ritual-circle anchor, exactly half the button height. It swaps to the shared gray cost-button skin while disabled. Other purchase cost buttons stay green and popup tabs stay on the brown selected/deselected skins.

The Bag dialog uses Title Case for its title, category tabs, currencies, and item names. `Currencies` and `Seeds` are always available; `Herbs` appears when Garden unlocks, while `Potions` and `Ingredients` appear when Brewing unlocks. Its retained category tabs keep the shared compact `11px` popup-tab type on the wider frame-attached strip.

The retained `Summoning Seeds` dialog uses Title Case throughout its visible title and content. Before Auto Summon research, it hides the automation paper and lets the seed-list paper fill the complete dialog body. Once researched, the top paper shows `Auto Summon` and `Keep Mana Above` at the same `15px` source text size, a `26px` gray-steel settings cog, the compact `60x24px` shared Root Run toggle, and the continuous mana-reserve slider with a mana value icon. The automation label follows the gear, while `Keep Mana Above` aligns to the slider's left edge. The toggle and mana value share the right edge, and the lowered reserve row sits close above a rail whose endpoint aligns beneath the mana count. The automation content keeps deliberate top padding. The first dialog open after the live locked-to-unlocked transition shrinks the seed paper into its settled lower slot while the automation paper pops from `0.8x` through a restrained `1.02x` overshoot, with every automation-paper node kept at full alpha. Later opens use the settled layout, and reduced motion snaps directly to it. Seed rows use the standard confirmed light tap haptic and a centered `0.97x` press followed by a restrained `1.035x` release peak before settling at `1x`; the complete row remains at full alpha. Tapping a seed expands that row over `240ms` and reveals its snapping drop-rate slider underneath with a full-alpha `0.92x` to `1.035x` to `1x` rubber snap; tapping it again collapses the row, and opening another seed transfers the disclosure without adding a selected/checkmarked state. Reduced motion snaps the press and disclosure directly to their final states. Both controls use the shared 14px slider rail. The drop-rate slider maps `none`, `low`, `medium`, and `high` to empty, red, yellow, and green fill while keeping the exact Root Run Settings knob. Those state names use matching contrast-safe text hues; `none` keeps the normal dialog ink. Every stop remains selectable, and choosing `none` for every seed makes summoning unavailable until one seed is set to an active weight. In that recoverable state, the gray summon button remains pressable and emits the centered stroked `Select a seed to drop` flyout without spending mana or playing the summon effect.
