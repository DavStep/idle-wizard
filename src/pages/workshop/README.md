# Workshop Page

The Workshop is the first room view.

It sits between Garden and Research. It shows one `Elara's Request` at a time, plus the summon action, bag popup, transient reward flyouts, discoveries, leaderboard, alliance controls, and page name. The request title is a passive label and never opens an information dialog. Completing the final request advances the player level automatically; the progression slot never becomes a separate level-up action. Segmented request progress and the Root Run one-star completion flyout live in the shared top panel alongside mana, coin, crystal, and level. The first-run reveal gate keeps that progress row hidden until Elara introduces requests. Prestige is its own gated page after level 7. Workshop secondary actions unlock by player level; keep those gates in page managers and keep gameplay effects behind gameplay/backend facades.

Follow `docs/style.md`: high-contrast text, quiet surfaces, readable Lilita One text, simple non-dialog boxes, and no decorative visuals. Reuse shared row, popup, border-label, and expandable-box patterns from `docs/ui-patterns.md`.

The Workshop side controls use the approved `RootRunSideAction` in two fixed stacks beginning `18px` below `Elara's Request`. Every control declares only a `left`/`right` side and numeric weight; the retained page sorts visible controls by weight and packs them into fixed source-space slots without leaving gaps for hidden controls. Visibility changes fade, slide from the owned edge, and settle at the fixed slot in at most `200ms`; reduced motion snaps directly to the final layout. Each action keeps a `50x60px` source hit box while rendering its flat illustrated icon at `72%` inside the `50x50px` art frame and nudging the artwork `2px` toward its owned screen edge. Centered Title Case `13.5px` Lilita One text follows at a tight `-10px` icon-to-label offset with a heavy rounded near-black outline. Long labels shrink uniformly within a `58px` visual width. Notification dots attach to the icon's top-right corner and optional timers sit beneath the label. The `62px` row pitch keeps the stacks compact while leaving separate tap areas. The shared brown, tan, gold, and purple icon family is: plain intact Alliance flag, Leaderboard podium, Tasks checklist with quill, Bag satchel, Stats star ledger, Inbox sealed envelope, Discoveries telescope, and Event calendar wand.
