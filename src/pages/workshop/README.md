# Workshop Page

The Workshop is the first room view.

It sits between Garden and Research. It shows one `elara's request` at a time, plus the summon action, bag popup, transient reward flyouts, discoveries, leaderboard, alliance controls, and page name. After all requests for a level are complete, the same progression slot becomes a separate, coin-free `level up` action. Segmented request progress and completion flights live in the shared top panel alongside mana, coin, crystal, and level. The first-run reveal gate keeps that progress row hidden until Elara introduces requests. Prestige is its own gated page after level 7. Workshop secondary actions unlock by player level; keep those gates in page managers and keep gameplay effects behind gameplay/backend facades.

Follow `docs/style.md`: high-contrast text, quiet surfaces, readable Lilita One text, simple non-dialog boxes, and no decorative visuals. Reuse shared row, popup, border-label, and expandable-box patterns from `docs/ui-patterns.md`.

The Workshop side controls form a fixed top stack directly below `elara's request`: `stats` sits in the top-right slot, then the left and right icon columns share one first-row baseline. The `bag` secondary action is the fourth left-side HUD panel button and uses the dedicated backpack art. Workshop HUD controls, including `summon seed`, `bag`, and `stats`, keep lowercase labels like the other side-panel buttons. `inbox` uses the matching honey-cream envelope and terracotta wax-seal art. `leaderboard` uses the matching warm-gold, black-outlined trophy art. `discoveries` uses the matching leather journal, magnifying glass, and gold-corner art. `stats` uses the shared yellow Root Run button configuration. Keep cost buttons on the green cost skin and popup tabs on the brown selected/deselected skins.
