# Market Page

The Market is the room page immediately right of Research. Its internal page id is still `shop` for save and navigation compatibility.

It has `traders`, `players`, and `crystals` tabs. The `crystals` tab is third and shows a manual coin offer plus real-money crystal bundles. Each offer reuses the stall-card composition with a named title, currency icon and overlaid amount in the left art well, plus a fixed green price or `Collect` action on the right. Pressing a price shows the support-unavailable popup. Payment and crystal grant behavior should stay out of this page until that flow is explicitly requested.

The `traders` tab contains `your stalls` and the Market Ledger. Tap a stall to open the item loader. Seeds are always available; Herbs appears after Garden unlocks and Potions appears after Brewing unlocks. Each category lists only researched or discovered items. Load Stall rows show the current trader unit price on the right; rows with owned, currently tradeable stock carry the shared red notification badge, and each item-kind tab rolls up the same state from its visible rows. Tapping an inventory row selects the current item without moving stock. The shared 14px slider rail with a draggable knob snaps through exact item counts from `0` through all matching stock already loaded plus matching stock still in inventory; `mark xN` reconciles that quantity once. `clear` returns loaded stock and stops future marking. `mark future` separately keeps the selected item assigned and routes every newly produced copy to that stand until stopped, including copies that finish after the dialog closes. It does not consume already-owned stock when enabled. Successful mark, clear, and future actions close the loader. A stand accepts only one loaded or future-marked item type at a time. Each stall reserves the same two-line row height. Loaded rows keep the item, batch size, and current sale value on the first line, with the sale progress rail and remaining timer together on the second line. When buyer demand reaches zero, that rail is replaced by a next-merchants countdown until the paused sale can resume. Empty future-marked rows show what they are waiting for.

Load Stall, Market Ledger, and other whole-dialog Market tab rows use the shared in-shell footer: `6px` of brown above, `10px` below, and equal count-derived widths (`8px` gaps for three tabs).

The ledger groups seeds, herbs, and potions while showing trader prices, stock, buyer need, and recent price history; it also owns trader purchases. Recent-history blocks keep visible separation, and an unchanged price reads `0 / Nh` without a direction or sign. The `players` tab contains local player requests and player-to-player listings. Both station-title sections reuse the retained Traders stand widget for every request/listing slot, including its independent card, art well, quantity/price fields, whole-card press feedback, semantics, and keyed pooling. `clear`, `browse market`, and `trade history` stay on the final card's lower border; claimable proceeds use a separate compact row with a normal green action inside the player-market section. `browse market` uses `selling` and `buying` popup tabs; `selling` shows backend public listings, while `buying` shows backend public request rows. Buying rows can prefill a matching player listing from an empty stand when the local player owns the requested item. Direct request fulfillment is not implemented until escrow/delivery behavior is explicit.

Request and Sell reuse Load Stall's split-paper picker: the selected item and transaction controls stay in the upper paper, item rows stay in the lower paper, and item-kind tabs stay in the shared shell footer. Request uses brown inset `Coins Per Item` and `Max Quantity` integer fields. Sell uses the shared quantity slider from `1` through the selected item's available quantity, capped by the player-market limit, then one `Coins Per Item` integer field beneath it. Selecting a Sell item initially marks the full available amount, matching Load Stall's full-allocation default.

Keep the stalls and Market Ledger actions self-explanatory without separate info controls.

For deterministic stall-layout QA without a backend account, open `/src/dev/uiRecipes/market-stalls.html` on the shared Vite server. Use `/src/dev/uiRecipes/market-stalls-retained.html?stalls=single` for the retained one-stall composition.
