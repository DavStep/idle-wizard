# Research Page

Research is the room page immediately right of Workshop.

It renders research boxes from gameplay snapshots. It does not own research costs, completion rules, seed summon filtering, mana effects, summon multipliers, or potion brewing permissions.

Available research prices use the shared cost-button controller and Root Run qUIck cost-button visual: the authored green nine-slice rendered at a compact `281x169` target, Lilita One outlined value, left currency icon, and original content geometry. Unaffordable research keeps the normal card, normal copy, and green button while only the currency amount turns red. Locked research reuses the normal card, art-well, artwork, and copy colors beneath one `30%` black overlay, then uses the gray cost button with white outlined `Locked` text plus a compact unlock requirement underneath. Research snapshots provide the formatted cost and `canResearch` state; the page only presents that state and forwards valid presses to `GameplayFacade.buyResearch`.

Each research uses a compact `80px` row, preserves the Root Run station-upgrade nine-slice margins, and fits the card between the shared content edges so the scroll viewport and right-edge scrollbar never clip it. The left art well is `52x52px`, its standard artwork is `57px`, and the cost/status button is `72x42px`. Seed unlock rows fit the shared seed-pack composite at `46px` so the complete sack and herb mark remain inset inside the art well, while recipe unlock rows use the exact potion inventory icon; other studies keep their research-family artwork. Active studies show a yellow status button with `Researching` on the top line and the centered light-gray live timer below it; completed studies replace that button with the shared checkmark centered in the same fixed status slot. Research labels are passive; only the cost button buys available research, while locked retained rows may show their compact requirement tooltip. The art, title, description, and action/status content band share a `3px` downward optical offset. Titles and descriptions use the same row ink color. Pixi descriptions fill the fixed middle region at the largest fitting source size from `11px` down to `8px`, then use a `-10px` optical offset so Lilita One's visible glyphs center against the art and action instead of its lower line box. Single-level studies do not show a level badge. True levelled studies, including research time, research cost, automation reserve, plot growth, cauldron brewing, and emerald room upgrades, keep their internal level labels for logs and requirements but show the stable research name plus the visual star rank in the row title. Two-level studies use two star slots; studies with three or more levels use three. `ResearchCardSkinManager` sizes each Pixi `NineSliceSprite` from the live DOM geometry so the compact row and well remain aligned with their CSS fallbacks. The DOM remains responsible for live text, artwork, accessibility, scrolling, and purchase taps. `npm run assets:research-skins` keeps whole-image CSS fallbacks for startup or WebGL failure; those fallbacks disappear only after the Pixi skin layer renders successfully. Idle Wizard intentionally omits Root Run's bottom `current ▶ next` capsule. Category boxes are unframed groups so the card art is never nested inside another panel. Incomplete research stays first and completed research sorts to the bottom. Completed rows are hidden by default. A category title shows the eye control only when that category contains completed research to hide; the control toggles only that category's completed rows for the current page session.

Successful research purchases reuse Root Run's station-upgrade feedback: a `360ms` whole-row overshoot/settle bounce, a masked additive shine across the card, and a faster masked shine across the cost button. The retained sprites and masks are reused between purchases, the shared seven-icon spend burst remains the currency feedback layer, and reduced-motion players receive the state and sound change without the shine or bounce.

Each category uses the approved `Research Station Title Plaque`: the Root Run `header-red` horizontal nine-slice geometry, connected directly to the screen's left edge at `42px` source height while the research cards keep their `16px` inset. Plaque color follows the owning research tab: Regular is yellow, Automation is red, Advanced is emerald green, and Crystal is purple. The title is Title Case in `18px` Lilita One. Its width is the measured rendered label plus the fixed decorated-cap allowance, capped by the right content edge. Keep `18px` between the previous category's final row and the next plaque, then `5px` between the plaque and its first row.
